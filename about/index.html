Built Around the Hobby

PokeVend 330 is an independent Akron-based business created to make sealed trading card products easier and more fun to discover.

OUR IDEA

More Ways to Collect

Trading card collecting is part product hunt, part nostalgia, and part excitement. PokeVend 330 brings that experience into places people already spend time, using modern cashless vending machines stocked with sealed products.

We are starting locally in Akron and focusing on doing one thing well: keeping machines clean, stocked, reliable, and worth checking whenever you walk by.

What Matters to Us

  • Authentic, sealed products
  • Reliable and responsive machine service
  • A rotating selection that keeps the experience fresh
  • Family-friendly presentation
  • Supporting local host businesses

Local
